# UBC final thesis and dissertation PDF requirements

Checked: 2026-09-17. Use official UBC pages as the source of truth. Recheck them for every final review if this snapshot is over 30 days old.

## Submission-critical checks

- Submit the final defended thesis or dissertation as a PDF compatible with Adobe Acrobat 5. It must not be secured or password-protected, and a scanned PDF is not acceptable.
- Check the internal PDF title. For final submission, use the all-lowercase filename `ubc_gradyear_gradmonth_lastname_firstname.pdf`, with underscores and no spaces. Vancouver students may select May or November; UBC's submission page reserves the February and September options for Okanagan students.
- Do not use blank pages. Use one-sided layout. Text pages must be US Letter (8.5 x 11 inches), single-column. Display landscape pages so they are readable without manual rotation.
- Minimum margins: left 1 inch; top, right, and bottom 0.75 inch. Recommended margins are 1.25 inches left and 1 inch elsewhere.
- Number the title page as `i` without displaying it; committee page `ii`; first abstract page `iii`. Use Arabic `1` on the first body page and continue through references and appendices. Display a number on every page except the title page.
- Place page numbers at least 0.5 inch from the edge. Lower centre or upper/lower right is allowed; lower right is preferred.

## Required order and front matter

Use this order when applicable:

1. Title page
2. Committee page
3. Abstract
4. Lay summary
5. Preface
6. Table of contents
7. List of tables
8. List of figures
9. List of submitted files
10. List of illustrations
11. Other lists or glossary
12. Acknowledgements
13. Dedication
14. Introduction, research chapters, and conclusion
15. Bibliography
16. Appendices

Apply these details:

- Committee page: second PDF page; no heading, signatures, or TOC entry. List all examining and supervisory committee members with their roles and affiliations.
  - Doctoral: include both University Examiners, update the page after the defence, omit the defence Chair, and treat External Examiner inclusion as optional.
  - Master's: use the master's committee template. List extra defence examiners as `Additional Examiner`; `University Examiner` is a doctoral-only role.
- Abstract: third PDF page, maximum 350 words, no tables/figures, and no separate keyword list. The cIRcle abstract must be identical.
- Lay summary: maximum 150 words and understandable to the public.
- Preface: include required authorship/collaboration, publication, copyright, and GenAI statements as applicable. Confirm GenAI use and wording with the author/supervisor.
- TOC: omit title and committee pages; include preliminary/end matter, all divisions, bibliography, and appendices. Right-align page numbers and use dot leaders.
- Lists of tables/figures/illustrations: start each on a new page; include number, title, page, and dot leaders. Add a submitted-files list when supplementary files exist.

## Body, figures, and end matter

- Start every chapter at the top of a new page. Distinguish heading levels clearly; keep numbering and capitalization consistent.
- Use one academic main-text font consistently. UBC normally expects the equivalent of 10-12 pt Times New Roman or Arial; text in tables and figures should be at least 2 mm high.
- Number tables and figures consistently and consecutively by one approved scheme. Use consistent captions. Repeat table headings on continuation pages. Keep reduced labels legible.
- Use one bibliography, start it at the top of a page, list it in the TOC, and do not assign it a chapter number. Keep web links functional and include retrieval dates unless the link is a DOI. UBC's component order exempts Master of Music and Master of Fine Arts in Creative Writing from the usual bibliography requirement.
- Start each appendix on a new page and list its title in the TOC. Remove signatures, personal information, and ethics certificates from the thesis or dissertation.
- Identify published and collaborative material in the text and Preface. State the student's contribution. Keep one document-wide bibliography when required and use consistent numbering, units, and abbreviations. Document required copyright permissions.

## Required human sign-off before GO

Require the author to confirm these fields in `author-info.yml`; PDF appearance cannot establish them reliably:

- Workday name, exact title, master's or doctoral level, degree, program, campus, graduation month/year, and final filename components.
- Final post-defence committee membership and roles. Apply the degree-specific committee rules above; ask doctoral students about the External Examiner and defence Chair rather than asking master's students irrelevant doctoral questions.
- Exact abstract and lay-summary word counts from the source text, and identity of the cIRcle abstract.
- Completion and approval of every minor revision and the program-approved style guide.
- Supervisor-approved GenAI disclosure; co-authorship contributions; copyright permissions; embargo and supplementary-file status.
- Removal of signatures, personal information, and ethics certificates.

Require a human visual pass to confirm:

- Every automated geometry flag on both the original render and UBC margin overlay at 240 DPI. Any formula, text, figure, table, caption, line, or glyph beyond a minimum body margin is a blocker; page numbers follow their separate 0.5-inch rule.
- At least one unflagged example of every equation, table, and figure layout family, plus every unusually wide equation or full-width object. This guards against low-contrast marks, unusual clipping/cropping, and other raster-detection edge cases.
- All visible page numbers, Roman-to-Arabic transition, component order, chapter openings, landscape orientation, and absence of blank pages.
- Legibility and completeness of every figure/table/equation/caption layout family, plus all unusually dense or full-page items.
- The final renamed PDF was reopened and is the exact file selected for cIRcle upload.

Do not issue `GO` while any margin hit, CropBox difference, page-number exception, or ambiguous low-contrast/full-width object remains unresolved. Automated geometry screening materially reduces risk but cannot replace this visual sign-off.
